About Shaam-e-Gazal

Created by Shivam Giri on August 13, 2026. Shaam-e-Gazal is a dedicated continuous online ghazal player bringing together timeless classics by Jagjit Singh, Pankaj Udhas, Begum Akhtar, Nusrat Fateh Ali Khan, Ghulam Ali, Talat Aziz, and Mehdi Hassan.

Frequently Asked Questions (FAQ)

What is Shaam-e-Gazal?
Shaam-e-Gazal is an intimate online mehfil streaming iconic Urdu and Hindi ghazals by legendary artists.
Who are the featured ghazal singers?
The playlist features Jagjit Singh, Chitra Singh, Pankaj Udhas, Begum Akhtar, Nusrat Fateh Ali Khan, Ghulam Ali, Talat Aziz, Hariharan, and Mehdi Hassan.
Is Shaam-e-Gazal free to listen to?
Yes, Shaam-e-Gazal is 100% free with no login or subscription required.

How to Enjoy the Evening Ghazal Mehfil

  1. Open Shaam-e-Gazal: Visit https://sham-e-gazal.netlify.app in any web browser.
  2. Press Play: Click the central Play button on the floating player card.
  3. Browse Playlist: Click the tracklist icon to view and select from 30 ghazals.
